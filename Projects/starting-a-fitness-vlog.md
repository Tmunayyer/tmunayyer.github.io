# Starting a Fitness Vlog
09/22/2024

Inspired by Sika Strength: [https://www.youtube.com/@sikastrength](https://www.youtube.com/@sikastrength)

Im starting this for similar reasons as I started live streaming myself learning. Its the most boring possible content but its a way to hold myself accountable and to focus on my goals.
## What
I don't struggle with consistency in the gym, but I do struggle with direction it seems. For the past few years I have been following programming that made me improve in areas that I find less important at the moment. This vlog is to keep me focused and provide a process of reflection.

Fitness goals:
- Squatting with no more knee pain
	- I have had minor to moderate knee pain for over a year now. I am working with a PT to resolve this.
- Match my back squat PB with a high bar back squat (was low bar) and no knee pain
	- If I could have anything right now fitness related it would be this. It would make me so happy.
- Unnoticeable strength discrepancy in my legs
	- At the moment my right leg is bigger than my left and is noticeably stronger. I am going to try and even these out.

With these goals in mind, the MVP of a fitness vlog will be a YouTube channel where I upload every single squat I do over the next three months. Ill do this once a week, likely Sunday, and voice over the video with an update/reflection on how things are going.

I'll call this project finish when I have an end to end process of recording, editing, and posting my squats for the week.
### Key Components

#### Recording: iPhone and Tripod
To keep things as simple as possible I'll just use my iPhone and a tripod to start. I already have both and the gym even has a tripod as well so I don't even have to take mine.

The one thing I don't like is my phone getting bloated with workout videos so I will have to be diligent about deleting old ones after they have been imported.
#### Editing
After some quick research, it seems like DaVinci Resolve will work for my needs. They have a paid version that has all the bells and whistles but I bet the free version will be able to handle trimming videos, laying audio tracks, and exporting as a single video easily. [Its on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/davinci-resolve-for-ipad/id1581363826).
## Requirements
For me to establish an MVP I should be able to:
- record myself in the gym
- upload, trim, and splice the videos together
- have a method of voicing over whatever I want to talk about
- be able to upload to YouTube
	- I already do this regularly thanks to the live stream learning project, not much to talk about here
## Research
### Video
The obvious choice is my iPhone. I bought a tripod just in case I needed one but the gym already has two. I also go to a CrossFit gym where its actually not outside the norm to record yourself. No one really cares. I think if it was a weird situation I would just give the people a heads up they might be in the video.

### Editing Video
For editing video I did some googling. Supposedly [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) is what professionals use, and their free version has tons of features. All I really need is the basics so free and professional work perfectly for me.

I could have use iMovie or whatever the one that is on the Mac is, but I have tried it in the past and wasn't super impressed. Might as well jump on the other one.

### Voice Overs
Voice memos on the iPhone can be shared via Apple's Airdrop super easily. For getting things started I think its going to work well. The one concern I had was if the audio format would be usable by everything or if it was some special format that only Apple uses and it would have to be converted.

Initial tests look like things work nicely and it was easy enough to import.
