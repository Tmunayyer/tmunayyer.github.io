### *La Maddalena*
**Finding the place**
- tiny island with a single real town
- got off the ferry and driving through the streets was super gnarly, absolutely tiny and narrow
- we ended up going around some of the more narrow streets and coming back down to our place
- we couldnt quite find the place since the GPS really wasnt helpful in the exact location, after some exploration we finally found it
- We saw a cat and immediately Amelie started making the clicking noise people who own cats make and I knew immediately that she owned a cat. She is low key obsessed I can tell but was keeping it cool.

**Accommodations**
- the airbnb was small but the big enough for the amount of time we actually spent in it
- some of the room had a super cool view looking over the town and across the sea
- in the morning it was especially nice
- it also had a balcony that was great to sit on and eat breakfast/relax before going out to dinner
- around us it felt like real people lived there and it wasnt just all vacation homes or airbnbs
- it was a super quick walk down to the harbor and main strip where all the restaurants and action was
- I had to sleep in the bunk beds

**Night of arrival**
- we got there a bit late, so first night was really just grabbing dinner
- we had a restaurant we planned to go to but I cant remember why we decided to keep looking
- luckily we did, ended up finding a little place tucked away up an alleyway off the main strip
- as soon as we saw it we knew we wanted to eat there just based on the vibes. It was super quiet and less busy than the other places
- the food ended up being pretty good. We got this pizza dish that was the thinest dough I think I have ever had. if I remember right we felt like it was a ripoff but the main courses were decent
- we then went to a gelato place that we passed on the way to dinner, pretty dang good
- when we got home, I remember I was pretty tired but brittany and sam do "couch time" where they sit on their phones and just chill. I normally would have just gone to bed but I think the excitement of arriving and FOMO made me stay up way longer than I normally would have. Amelie and AJ were out there too and we really just hung out
