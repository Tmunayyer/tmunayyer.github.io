At the gym I went to in Nashville, the free weekend Hyrox classes would have insane attendance. Dozens of people would show up to an unflashy crossfit gym to workout. The conversion to gym members was surprisingly low. Below are my thoughts on how you actually might capitalize on free Hyrox classes.


## Year Round Classes
You can offer generic year round classes. This is for people who love Hyrox. The problem is Hyrox seems to be a highly social activity. Few people are training Hyrox for the love of the sport and are more interested in being around other people. This means in order for year round classes to work, you need critical mass. If you don't have a compelling reason to get a large group started, attracting random new members will be difficult.

You have to find a compelling reason for people to show up solo, and that would be Competition preparation and working on weaknesses. From their you could even potentially branch into Hyrox Strength (normal strength classes but marketed correctly)
## Competition Prep
The most obvious path forward is to offer some sort of competition prep multiple days a week. People would start attending the classes so they could be ready for their local simulation or race. This would mostly be some sort of peaking program
